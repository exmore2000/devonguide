Getting there & around
  • By carDraft route idea: A376 from Exmouth towards Exeter, with stops at Darts Farm and Greendale. Check the route on a map first.
  • By trainExeter St Davids is the main rail hub. The Avocet Line runs to Exmouth, with local trains also serving Topsham. The Tarka Line leaves from Exeter for Barnstaple.
  • By busThe Jurassic Coast 9A (Stagecoach) runs Seaton, Sidmouth and Exeter via Sidford, Newton Poppleford and Farringdon. It is generally hourly from Seaton Monday to Saturday, and about every 30 minutes between Sidmouth and Exeter where it shares the route with the 9 from Honiton. It also runs on Sundays and bank holidays at a reduced frequency. Exeter Bus Station has services to Okehampton, Moretonhampstead and beyond.
  • By bikeThe Exe Estuary Trail suits a cycling pub crawl between Exeter, Topsham and Exmouth, with bike hire nearby.
  • On footThe South West Coast Path and the Jurassic Coast run along the East Devon shore. Topsham has the estuary path, and many of the pubs and farm shops here are within walking distance of the bus or train.
  • TaxiTaxis serve Exeter, Exmouth, Sidmouth and Seaton. Book ahead if you're travelling out to a rural farm shop or brewery.

See Getting around for general tips. Timetables, fares and seasons change, so check with the operator or Traveline South West before you travel.

Farm shops & producers
  • Bartlett's Farm ShopNear Sidmouth. Baking, dairy, grocery, a meat counter and a cafe.
  • Darts FarmTopsham EX3 0QH. Won Best Farm Shop and Best Farm Restaurant at the 2025 Farm Retail Awards.
  • Forest Fungi & The Farm ShopNear Sidmouth. Bakery, cafe and preserves.
  • Greendale Farm ShopSidmouth Road, near Farringdon, Exeter EX5 2JU. Farm shop, café, farm trails, events and its own Greend'ale. Now also running Occombe Farm in Paignton.
  • Joshuas Harvest StoreNear Sidmouth. Grocery, meat, preserves and a cafe.
  • Kenniford Farm ShopNear Sidmouth. Preserves, meat and dairy; listed as a high-welfare pork supplier.
  • Knight Farm ShopNear Sidmouth. Preserves, meat, dairy and baking.
  • Millers Farm ShopNear Axminster. Local produce for over 40 years, with a French-inspired range.
  • Otterton MillRestored watermill on the Jurassic Coast that grinds its own flour. Bakery, preserves and a cafe.
  • Quicke's Cheese ShopNewton St Cyres, near Crediton. Clothbound cheddar from a historic farm.
  • Trill Farm, near AxminsterMixed organic farm with an education centre, meat and preserves.

Breweries
  • The Exeter BreweryCowley Bridge Road, Exeter EX4 4NX. Taproom bar. Beers include Ferryman, Lighterman, Fall's Over and Darkness.
  • Powderkeg BreweryWoodbury Salterton EX5 1PY. Beers include Speak Easy, Hops & Dreams, Chequered Past, the low-alcohol Green Light and gluten-free Cut Loose pilsner.
  • Otter BreweryLuppitt, Blackdown Hills. Founded 1990, and opened its first pub, The Holt, in Exeter in 2025. Beers include Otter Bright, Otter Head, Otter Elf and Tarka lager.
  • Topsham BreweryExeter area. Beers include Goat Walk pale ale, Ask Your Dad bitter and the Belgian-style Prepare for Trouble!
  • Beer EngineNewton St Cyres. Known for strong mild Old Iron Horse.
  • Hanlons BreweryHalf Moon, near Newton St Cyres. Beers include Simcoe New IPA.
  • Crossed Anchors BreweryExmouth. Community brewery, with American Pale Ale, 3Cs Gold and Exe Porter.
