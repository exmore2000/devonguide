Latest · updated 23 November 2026
  • Exeter park and ride fares stay low for Christmas shoppers23 Nov. The Sowton service into the city keeps its cut price of £3.50 return, with buses about every 15 minutes Monday to Saturday and closed on Christmas Eve until 29 December. Devon Air Radio
  • Late-night bar plan for a former Fore Street venue in Exeter22 Nov. A licence request would bring the old nightclub premises back into use under a new name, with later opening hours. The council will decide. Devon Air Radio
  • Exeter's Wonford sports centre to close for about 18 months20 Nov. The city council plans a bigger gym, a refurbished sports hall and a new café, with work due to start in the new year. Members can use other Exeter leisure centres meanwhile. Devon Air Radio
  • Sidmouth asks locals about the future of Fisherman's Yard20 Nov. Two drop-in sessions are planned, on 27 and 30 November, to hear views on an early concept for replacing the tired buildings. Devon Air Radio

  • Crowdfunder launched for a Chagford vegetable plot20 Nov. The community-run plot supplies food to around 120 local families, and the appeal aims to keep it going. Tavistock Today
  • Exeter Racecourse reopens after a delayed start to the season19 Nov. Racing was held up by dry ground, and recent rain means jump racing can begin on Sunday 22 November. The Exeter Daily
  • Young singers from across Devon to perform with an orchestra at Exeter Cathedral19 Nov. About 250 pupils will join the Gabrieli ensemble on 24 November as part of a national singing tour. The Exeter Daily
  • Tickets on sale for a 2027 charity bike ride from Topsham19 Nov. FORCE Cancer Charity is marking its 40th year with a 60 or 100 mile ride on 27 June 2027. The Exeter Daily
  • Free festive parking for Tavistock shoppers19 Nov. West Devon council is offering free parking in the town over the Christmas period. Tavistock Today
