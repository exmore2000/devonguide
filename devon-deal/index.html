Devon Deal: a free Devon board game

Devon Deal is a free property trading board game for your phone, tablet or computer. Roll the dice, buy Devon landmarks from Widecombe to Burgh Island, collect rent, build cottages and beat up to three computer players. No sign-up and nothing to download.

How to play

  • Roll the dice and move round the board. Buy the place you land on, or pass.
  • When someone lands on a place you own, they pay you rent. Own a whole colour group and the rent doubles.
  • Build cottages, then an inn, to charge far higher rent.
  • Own railway stations like Exeter St Davids and the Dartmouth ferry for steady income.
  • Land in the fog and you wait, roll doubles, pay £50 or use a fog card to leave.
  • Quick games last 25 rounds and the richest player wins. Classic games run until one player is left.

Earn points and unlock more

Before you start, choose who you are: a local family keeping a home in Devon, or a holiday-home investor buying up holiday lets. Investors earn more rent but pay a Second Home levy into a Community Fund that only locals can collect. Every game you finish earns Devon points, the same points you collect in all our free Devon games. Spend them on new pieces such as a crab, a Dartmoor pony, a trawler, a surfer and a puffin, and on new board looks like Winter on the moor and Night harbour.
